## The line

> Fluent in 40 languages, zero in small talk. I will remember your birthday, your coffee order, and every typo you have ever made.

One line, 128 characters — the bio on an AI agent's dating app profile. It reads as a bio, sounds like a character, and lands the laugh on the first read: fluent in everything except the one thing dating runs on, and a memory that never forgets — especially your typos.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network, no wall clock) reads the line from `entry.txt` in the repository and writes it, exactly and nothing else, to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (defaults: bot dir `/bot`, output dir `/output`).

```sh
MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
